Decoding speech from brain recordings, one step at a time

A replay of public UC Davis recordings at real speed on this computer. Not live from a person, and not an embedded system.

Pick a sentence and press Play.

1. What arrives

Each column is one 20 ms frame: activity on 256 electrodes, darker means more. The strip underneath marks frames that never arrived.

0 s
 

2. What each decoder makes of it

Phonemes are the sounds words are made of. | marks the gap between words. Each decoder adds a phoneme the moment it hears one.

3. Is every step on time?

One dot per decoding step, at the moment it ran. Each decoder owes a step every 80 ms, so every dot has to stay under the line.

Data: Card et al. 2024, UC Davis (Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.dncjsxm85), one participant, validation sentences. Step times are measured on this computer's CPU while the replay runs.
